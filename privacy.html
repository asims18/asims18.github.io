Privacy policy

This policy applies to the app financial-ledger-backup.

Who uses the app

Only the owner of the app uses it. The app is not for other people.

What the app does with Google data

  • The app uses the Google Drive scope drive.file. With this scope, the app can see only the files that it made.
  • The app writes encrypted backup files to the Google Drive of the owner, and reads them to restore a backup.
  • The app does not read other files in Google Drive.

What the app collects

The app collects no data about other people. It does not share data with other parties. It does not sell data.

Contact

To ask a question about this policy, open an issue on the GitHub profile of the owner.
